## §1. Matching the greybody-factor amplitude to the PN inspiral

Continuity of $H$ and $d\ln H/d\ln\omega$ at $\omega_m$ fixes both parameters of the greybody-factor amplitude in closed form, with no NR input.

The model is $H=H_{\rm PN}$ for $\omega<\omega_m$ and $H=M_fA\sqrt{\mathcal R(M_f\omega,\chi_f)}\,(M_f\omega)^{-p}$ above. Continuity gives

$$p=-\frac{d\ln H_{\rm PN}}{d\ln\omega}\Big|_{\omega_m}+\frac12\frac{d\ln\mathcal R}{d\ln\bar\omega}\Big|_{M_f\omega_m},\qquad A=\frac{H_{\rm PN}(\omega_m)(M_f\omega_m)^{p}}{M_f\sqrt{\mathcal R(M_f\omega_m)}} .$$

For LO PN the first term is $7/6$. Below the knee $\mathcal R\approx1$, so $p\approx7/6$ unless $\omega_m$ lies within about $0.2\,\omega_{\rm LR}$ of the light ring.

## §2. The amplitude as radiated angular momentum per unit frequency

The stationary-phase amplitude is fixed by $\lvert dJ_{22}/d\Omega\rvert$ alone; a constant $dJ/d\Omega$ through the merger (BOB) gives $p=\tfrac12$.

For the $(2,\pm2)$ pair the energy spectrum is $dE/d\omega=\omega^2H^2/(16\pi^2)$. A quasi-circular chirp radiates with $dE=\Omega\,dJ$, and $\omega=2\Omega$. Writing $\mathcal J'=\lvert dJ_{22}/d\Omega\rvert$,

$$\frac{dE}{d\omega}=\frac{\Omega}{2}\,\mathcal J'\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H(\omega)=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\mathcal J'(\omega/2)}{\omega}} .$$

**Check.** The Newtonian $J=\eta\Omega^{-1/3}$ gives $\mathcal J'=\tfrac{\eta}{3}\Omega^{-4/3}$, and so $H=\pi\sqrt{2/3}\,\eta^{1/2}\Omega^{-7/6}$, the leading-order SPA amplitude.

**Merger.** BOB's news-frequency relation keeps $dJ/d\Omega$ constant through the merger. Then $H\propto\omega^{-1/2}$, so the greybody-factor exponent is $p=\tfrac12$. NR fits give $0.3$–$0.8$, with median $0.42$.

**What NR shows.** $d\ln\mathcal J'_{\rm NR}/d\ln\omega$ (median of 60 cases) goes from $-1.5$ at $0.25\,\omega_{\rm LR}$ to $-0.3$ at $0.65$–$0.75\,\omega_{\rm LR}$; above that the greybody factor cuts it off. The flattening is gradual, so a sharp freeze is only an approximation.
