We solve a toy model of second-order perturbation theory on the Pöschl–Teller potential. The first-order field starts from initial data localized at $(t_0, r_0)$, so it is the Green's function, $\phi_1(t, r) = G(t, t_0; r, r_0)$. The second-order field obeys the same wave equation with the source $\delta(r - r_s)\,\phi_1^2$. At second-order, we find the time at which quadratic QNMs are excited, and the presence of 
quadratic Matsubara modes, as well as QNMs driven by Matsubara modes. We compare analytical results against numerical evolutions in the time domain.
