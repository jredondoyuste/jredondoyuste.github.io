We solve a toy model of second-order perturbation theory on the Pöschl–Teller potential. The first-order field starts from initial data localized at $(t_0, r_0)$, so it is the Green's function, $\phi_1(t, r) = G(t, t_0; r, r_0)$. The second-order field obeys the same wave equation with the source $\delta(r - r_s)\,\phi_1^2$. Naritaka Oshita and Daiki Saito have written an analytical solution for the second-order field in all of spacetime, as a sum over quasinormal modes (QNMs), Matsubara modes, quadratic QNMs (QQNMs) and their products.

We ask whether that solution is correct, first by checking the derivation, then by evolving the equations in the time domain with thin Gaussians in place of the deltas and comparing with the mode sums once enough modes are included. It is a first step towards the full second-order response, beyond the quadratic QNMs alone.
