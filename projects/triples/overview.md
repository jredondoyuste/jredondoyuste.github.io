Black holes grow and merge in complex environments. This opens the possibility of triple systems, and a particular kind of triples dubbed binary extreme mass ratio inspirals or bEMRIs for short. The small binary in a bEMRI may merge in the strong-field region of a supermassive black hole, potentially showing interesting relativistic features. In this work we try to bridge the gap between perturbative descriptions of bEMRIs and numerical relativity simulations at moderate mass ratios, as well as estimating the astrophysical rate at which these events may occur.
