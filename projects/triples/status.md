**Starting (2026-10-07).** We follow up [arXiv:2605.20320](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20320) with bEMRI-like triples in numerical relativity: a small binary merging very close to a supermassive black hole. The project has just been set up; Giuseppe Ficarra is running the first NR simulations, and the three lines of work below are not yet opened as tasks.

## Tasks

### NR data tools and GW emission · planned

Tools to read the NR data and understand the GW emission, including resonant excitation of the SMBH's modes.

### 2.5PN triples for NR initial data · planned

Evolve triples up to 2.5PN with our SpaceHub fork to guess a good initial configuration for the NR code.

### Astrophysical rate · planned

Estimate how often these events occur at the mass ratios, distances, etc. that we consider.
