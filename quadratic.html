Quadratic scattering

Suppose two gravitational waves hit a Schwarzschild black hole. Gravity is nonlinear, so the black hole responds with a third gravitational wave at the sum of their frequencies. The strength of this nonlinear response is the quadratic coupling 𝒬 we computed in Bucciotti, Redondo-Yuste, Zhong, Kuntz & Cardoso (2026). How this is computed

The couplings were computed with BlackHoleNonlinearities.jl, which implements the calculation of the paper, for ℓ ≤ 6 and on a grid of frequencies ωi = x Re ωℓi0, x = 0.05, 0.10, …, 1.30. This page only reads that table; the sliders step through the grid, without interpolating.
