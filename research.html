Strong Gravity & Gravitational Waves

I explore the nonlinear dynamics of spacetime and matter under extreme conditions, such as when two black holes or neutron stars merge. My work combines analytical techniques with numerical simulations, keeping an eye on improving our tools for gravitational wave data analysis. Click here to read more about my research interests on black holes and neutron stars.

I am always eager to collaborate on projects related to strong-field gravity and gravitational waves. If you are also excited about these topics, and especially if you are a student, please reach out!
