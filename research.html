black hole perturber

Gravity fascinates me, especially when it is highly nonlinear. My work explores nonlinear effects in the relaxation of black holes. I’m also intrigued by how astrophysical environments might absorb, reflect, trap, or amplify gravitational waves. When I have more fun is when mathematics, numerics, and approximations come together to reveal new insights.

Below are some of my favorite recent papers. For the full list, see my profile in Spires or Google Scholar.
